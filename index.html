Background

As a PhD researcher, I focus on Internet of Things and User Interfaces. My interdisciplinary background — from design through computer science to my doctoral studies — enables me to tackle technical challenges with human-centered thinking.

With a Master's degree in Computer Science and professional training in design, I bring both technical depth and design sensibility to my research. I develop IoT systems that are not only functional but also intuitive and user-friendly.
